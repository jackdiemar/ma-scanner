What We Do

Black Starlight Capital monitors biotech and specialty finance for documentary evidence of strategic deal processes before the market fully prices them in. The core work is systematic: reading SEC filings, detecting process signals, tracking signal sequences, and filtering noise from names with genuine strategic process activity.

Our approach is systematic but not mechanical. Every model output is a starting point for deeper diligence, not a conclusion. We believe the edge in modern markets is not access to information — it is the ability to structure that information into a coherent, repeatable investment thesis faster and more rigorously than others.

Methodology

The system scans 500+ biotech names daily across seven layers, but the actionable output is process-state classification — not a score. Names are tracked through four states: Live Process (active SA review, activist 13D, banker retained), Pathway (ROFR/ROFN clause present), Aging (signal older than 90 days without new evidence), and Screening (high attractiveness score, no process evidence). Score-only names are explicitly capped and not presented as trade candidates.

The mREIT Scanner monitors the spread between net asset value and market price in mortgage REITs, cross-referenced against Fed policy trajectory, duration exposure, and insider positioning — isolating situations where rate expectations and book value diverge in ways the market has not yet corrected.

Philosophy

We do not trade noise. We do not chase momentum. We build positions in situations where the structural setup — pipeline readout windows, patent cliff pressure on acquirers, book value dislocations — creates a defined catalyst path within a visible time horizon. Discipline over activity. Precision over volume.
